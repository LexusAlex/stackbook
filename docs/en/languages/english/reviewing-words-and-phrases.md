# Reviewing words and phrases

**Have 5 minutes? Start with the two phrases below.** Listen, hide the answers and try to recall them yourself. The cards are ready: you do not need to install anything or copy them out.

After [Goal and starting point](./goal-and-starting-point), the phrase for giving your name is familiar. If you have forgotten it, start with the model.

## 1. Open two cards — one minute

Click each card heading to read the phrase and translation. Use your own name in the name card. You can leave the answers open for now.

::: details 1. Say hello

**Hi!** — «Привет!»

Example: **Hi, Anna!** — «Привет, Анна!»

:::

::: details 2. Give your name

**My name's Alex.** — «Меня зовут Алекс».

Replace Alex with your name, for example: **My name's Anna.** — «Меня зовут Анна».

:::

## 2. Listen and repeat — one minute

Press **▶** and listen to the man's first turn. He says hello and gives his name: **Hi, my name's Peter.** Then pause.

Repeat **Hi!** and **My name's …** with your name. Replay the opening if needed. You do not need the whole conversation today.

## 3. Hide and recall — one minute

1. Click both card headings again to hide the English answers.
2. Look at **Give your name** and try saying the phrase. Give yourself about five seconds.
3. Now open the card and check. If you forgot, read the answer aloud and hide it again.
4. Check **Say hello** in the same way. Then try giving your name once more.

The key action: **try to recall → then reveal the answer**. Reading an open card is a different exercise.

## 4. Use the phrases — one minute

Close the cards. Imagine meeting Anna. Say hello and give your name. Then write those two phrases in your usual notes app or on paper.

::: details Compare with a model

For someone called Alex:

> Hi, Anna! My name's Alex.

Use your own name. If you forgot a phrase, open its card, repeat it and try again.

:::

## 5. Finish and return tomorrow — one minute

Two lines in your note are enough. For example:

```text
Recalled on my own: 1 of 2. With help: the name phrase.
Next review: tomorrow, before a new lesson.
```

Count only answers given **before opening the card**. If you looked first and then answered correctly, that is an answer with help. You do not need to update the count after correcting it.

**Enough for today:** you tried recalling two phrases and used them to introduce yourself. If five minutes ran out earlier, continue from the remaining step next time.

Check the same two cards tomorrow. If you recall both, review them in three days, then a week after the next successful check. If you forget a phrase, return to it tomorrow. If you miss a day, continue at your next session.

You can move on to [The English alphabet](./alphabet) while keeping up short reviews.

## For another session: three more cards

Once the first two phrases are familiar, add **one new card per session**. Follow the same order: read → listen → hide → recall → check.

::: details 3. Ask the other person's name

**What's your name?** — «Как вас зовут?»

Example: **What's your name? — My name's Anna.** — «Как вас зовут?» — «Меня зовут Анна».

In the recording, the question is in Peter's first turn.

:::

::: details 4. Say you are pleased to meet someone

**It's nice to meet you.** — «Приятно познакомиться».

Example: **It's nice to meet you, Anna.** — «Приятно познакомиться, Анна».

In the recording, the phrase comes near the beginning, after the greetings. You can find it in the **Transcript** and listen with the text.

:::

::: details 5. Ask where the other person is from

**Where are you from?** — «Откуда вы?»

Example: **Where are you from, Anna?** — «Откуда вы, Анна?»

In the recording, Peter asks Carla this question towards the end. Find it in the **Transcript**, then listen to that part.

:::

If you want paper cards later, put the task on one side and the phrase and example on the other. Keep a link to this lesson with them: the audio is here.
