# Reviewing words and phrases

**About 5 minutes: look at two phrases, hide them and recall them yourself.** The cards, audio and answer fields are already here.

## 1. Open two cards

Click a heading and read the phrase and translation. You can look at the answer for now.

::: details 1. Say hello

**Hi!** — «Привет!»

Example: **Hi, Anna!** — «Привет, Анна!»

:::

::: details 2. Give your name

**My name's Alex.** — «Меня зовут Алекс».

Replace Alex with your name: **My name's Anna.** — «Меня зовут Анна».

:::

## 2. Listen and repeat

Press **▶**. In his first turn, the man says hello and gives his name. Pause after it and repeat **Hi! My name's …** with your name.

## 3. Hide and recall

Click both card headings again to hide the answers. Try saying the phrase first, then type it below. If you forgot, open the card and try again.

**Enough for today.** The key action: recall first → reveal the answer afterwards. You do not have to get everything right immediately.

Review these two phrases here tomorrow. If you recall them, return in three days, then a week after a successful review. If you forget, try again tomorrow. You can continue to [The English alphabet](./alphabet) while keeping up reviews.

## For another session: three more cards

Add one new phrase at a time. Read it, find it in the recording above and repeat it aloud. Then hide the card, look only at the task and try answering.

::: details 3. Ask the other person's name

**What's your name?** — «Как вас зовут?»

Example: **What's your name? — My name's Anna.** — «Как вас зовут?» — «Меня зовут Анна».

The question is in the man's first turn in the player above.

:::

::: details 4. Say you are pleased to meet someone

**It's nice to meet you.** — «Приятно познакомиться».

Example: **It's nice to meet you, Anna.** — «Приятно познакомиться, Анна».

The phrase comes near the beginning of the same recording, after the greetings.

:::

::: details 5. Ask where the other person is from

**Where are you from?** — «Откуда вы?»

Example: **Where are you from, Anna?** — «Откуда вы, Анна?»

In the same recording, the man asks Carla this question towards the end.

:::
