Both words have the letter **a**, but it sounds different: /æ/ and /eɪ/. The final **e** in cake is not pronounced separately. These observations apply to these words; check a new word's sound with audio.

## 2. Separate letters and sounds

| What you see | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **cat** | The spelling of the word meaning «кошка» |
| **C — A — T** | Its separate letter names: /siː/ — /eɪ/ — /tiː/ |
| **/kæt/** | A written representation of the whole word's pronunciation |

We see and write letters; we hear and produce sounds. The pronunciation between slashes is called a **transcription**. Name C, A and T when spelling the word; say the whole word as in the first player.

<LessonCheck lang="en" question="What does /kæt/ show?" :options="['Three letter names', 'The whole word pronunciation', 'The word translation']" :correct="1" explanation="It is a transcription — a written representation of how to pronounce cat." />
