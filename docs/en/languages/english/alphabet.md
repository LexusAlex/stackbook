# The English alphabet

**About 5 minutes: listen to letters and say the letters in your name.** The players, table and checks are here. Start with Alex, then try your own name.

## 1. Listen to the alphabet

Press **▶**: the recording lasts about 31 seconds. Open the table below and follow the letters. On a second listen, pause and repeat small groups: **A–G**, **H–N**, **O–T**, **U–Z**.

## 3. Say the letters in your name

Enter your name in Latin letters or keep Alex. Say the English letter names, using the audio and table above. Click **Hide model**, repeat without it and reveal it to check the order.

<LessonName mode="alphabet" lang="en" />

**Anna** has four letters: **A — N — N — A**. Say N twice. Here, name each letter separately; pronouncing a whole word is the [next lesson](./letters-and-sounds).

**Enough for today:** you recognised a capital letter and tried naming the letters in your name. If five minutes were not enough, continue from here next time.
